⚕️ Health emergency

📞 Call ambulance (108)

CPR — if someone is unresponsive and not breathing normally

  1. Check response: tap shoulders, shout. If no response, call 108 immediately (or ask someone else to).
  2. Position: lay them flat on their back on a firm surface.
  3. Hand placement: heel of one hand on the center of the chest, other hand on top, fingers interlocked.
  4. Compressions: push hard and fast, about 5–6 cm deep, at 100–120 compressions/min (roughly the beat of "Stayin' Alive"). Let the chest fully recoil between pushes.
  5. Continue until medical help arrives or the person starts breathing/moving. If trained and an AED is available, use it as soon as possible.
  6. If you're not trained in rescue breaths, hands-only CPR (compressions only, no breaks) is still effective and recommended.

Choking

  1. If they can cough or speak, encourage coughing — don't intervene yet.
  2. If they can't breathe/cough/speak: give 5 back blows between the shoulder blades, then 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver), alternating until the object clears or help arrives.

Fainting / dizziness

  1. Help them lie down and raise their legs slightly, or sit with head between knees.
  2. Loosen tight clothing, ensure airflow, don't crowd them.
  3. If they don't recover within a minute or two, or have chest pain/confusion, call 108.

This is general guidance, not a substitute for certified first-aid training.
